Nastro

Nastro is a lightweight, skinnable desktop music player. It plays the audio files on your computer, with no account, no cloud and no telemetry.

What it is

Three panels, the player, a 10-band equalizer and the playlist, dock together with magnetic snapping: drag one near another and it snaps to its edge, and the group moves as one. The look of all three is a skin, a small text file you can change or replace.

  • Local first. It plays files from your disk. Nothing leaves your machine, and there is nothing to sign in to.
  • Skinnable. Four built-in skins (Silver, Graphite, Porcelain and Nebula), your own skins in a plain skin.toml file, and support for classic skins is planned. See Skins.
  • Light. A native Rust application, not a web page in a window. An idle player does no work.
  • Open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Nastro is in early development. Linux comes first, and the Windows and macOS builds are not verified yet. Some pages of this guide are still being written.

Where to start

  • Installation: get Nastro for your system.
  • Quick start: open some music and press play.
  • Skins: change how Nastro looks.
  • Files and folders: where Nastro keeps its settings, playlists and logs.
  • Troubleshooting: no sound, logs and other problems.
